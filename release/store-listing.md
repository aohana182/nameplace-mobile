## Full description (4000 chars max)

You meet someone interesting at a conference. At a market. On a trail. By the time you get home, you can't remember their name, where you were, or what they told you.

Nameplace fixes that.

Long-press anywhere on the map where you met them. Add their name, a few notes, and a tag. Done. The pin is there the next time you open the app.

No sign-up. No cloud. No account. Your connections stay on your phone in a local database. Nothing is sent to any server. No ads, no telemetry, no tracking.

What you can do:
- Drop a pin wherever you met someone: a coffee shop, a park bench, a trade show floor
- Add a name, personal notes, and one or more tags (Friend, Work, Family, Neighbor, or your own)
- Tap a pin to see who you met and what you wrote
- Filter the map by tag to pull up all your Work contacts, or just your Neighbors
- Color-coded pins so you can read the map at a glance

Most apps store your data in the cloud. That means it lives on someone else's servers, gets shared with advertisers, or disappears when the company shuts down. Nameplace stores everything on your phone. Your data does not leave your device.

No signal needed. Underground, on a plane, in a dead zone, it works the same. It has nothing to check in with.

Saving a pin is instant. No spinner, no "saving..." message, no server round-trip. You tap Save and the pin is on the map.
