# Privacy Policy — Nameplace

*Last updated: June 2026*

---

## What Nameplace does

Nameplace is a map-based app that lets you record the people you meet and where you met them. You drop a pin on a map, add a name and notes, and assign tags.

---

## Data we collect

**None.**

Nameplace does not collect, transmit, or store any data on any server. There is no account system, no analytics, and no telemetry of any kind.

---

## Data stored on your device

All information you enter into Nameplace — pin locations, names, notes, and tags — is stored locally in a SQLite database on your phone. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not backed up to any cloud service
- Is not accessible to Nameplace or any third party
- Is deleted permanently when you uninstall the app

---

## Location permission

Nameplace requests access to your device's location to show your current position on the map and to place pins at your current location. This data:

- Is used only within the app, in real time
- Is never transmitted to any server
- Is never stored beyond what you explicitly save as a pin

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device Settings. The app will continue to function — you will simply need to place pins by long-pressing on the map manually.

---

## Third-party services

Nameplace uses **Google Maps** to render the map. Google's own privacy policy applies to map tile rendering. Nameplace does not pass any of your pin data or personal information to Google Maps — only your device's location (if permitted) is used to center the map.

Google Maps Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## Children

Nameplace does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since no information is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.
